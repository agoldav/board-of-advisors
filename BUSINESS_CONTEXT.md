## What We Do

**Core Business:** Installation of specialized building materials

- Polycarbonate roofs
- Composite aluminum facades
- Perforated aluminum
- Phenolic panels
- Louvers
- Occasional: other specialty materials

**Installation Model:** Subcontracted by builders/developers to install materials on their projects. We don't sell products; we sell installation labor + materials procurement.

**Customer Types:**

1. Large and small construction companies (subcontract us for their projects)
2. House developers (multiple simultaneous projects; we install small roofs across their portfolio)
3. End clients (individual homeowners with personal projects)

## Project Economics



### Eurohogar & Casamax (Core Business)

- **Project size:** $30,000–$40,000 per project
- **Project duration:** 3–4 months typical; can extend 1–2 years for large multi-home developments
- **Billing cycle:** Every 15 days
- **Payment terms:** Paid ~7 days after invoice (with occasional 1-week slip if invoice crosses month boundary)
- **Number concurrent:** 4–5 projects simultaneously



### Large Construction (Occasional)

- **Project size:** $50,000+ (rare)
- **Timeline:** 1 month expedited, or standard 3–4 months
- **Payment structure:** 
  - Upfront advance (material purchase)
  - Progress payments every 15 days or monthly
  - Retention (30% contract, 1–3 months in practice to release)
- **Impact:** Retentions delayed 2–3 months are the single largest cash-flow drag



### Small/Miscellaneous

- **Project size:** $1,000–$5,000
- **Frequency:** Occasional

## Competitive Position



### Strengths

- **Reliability:** We honor all warranties; we show up for guarantees
- **Customer loyalty:** 10+ year relationships with Eurohogar, Casamax
- **Quality of service:** Clients report satisfaction



### Weaknesses

- **Pricing:** We're expensive (discussed separately if needed); local procurement adds cost vs. imported competitors
- **Work quality:** Mediocre to average; most competitors are the same or worse, but I want to be best-in-class
- **Schedule adherence:** Understaff → miss deadlines on complex projects
- **Warranty costs:** High number of warranty callbacks (3–4 re-visits per project typical)
- **Operational overhead:** David spending hours on truck logistics instead of managing projects



### Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors (formal companies):**

- Polyacril (largest, oldest, backed by Grupo El Lagar; beats us on price and marketing)
- Policarbonatos de Costa Rica
- Alucom
- Ramdom
- Grupo Rolan

**Indirect competitors (informal):**

- Independent installers (former subcontractors, informal, can undercut price)

**Market dynamic:** Big projects go to Polyacril; we get mid-tier work. Informals eat at the low end.

## Operational Constraints



### Capacity Bottlenecks

1. **Personnel:** Only David can coordinate sites; he's also the truck driver. Overloaded.
2. **Vehicle:** One truck; serves 4–5 simultaneous projects 1+ hour apart. Logistics is a permanent constraint.
3. **Installer availability:** Aluminum work especially hard to staff; available installers want personal projects or higher pay
4. **David's time:** If he's in the truck, no one is managing projects. Quality and timeline suffer.



### Material Sourcing

- **Typical:** Buy in Costa Rica (local market procurement; higher cost)
- **Large projects:** Import for economies of scale (e.g., $50k project, might be worth it)
- **Ideal scenario:** If Eurohogar/Casamax volume justified it, importing every 4 months would improve margin significantly (estimated 4-month consumption if we imported)
